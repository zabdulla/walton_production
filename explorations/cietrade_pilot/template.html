How these numbers are made

  1. Each job owns the shifts that ran between the moment the office opened it and the moment the next job on that line was opened — one day while jobs were opened every morning (through Aug 21), several days since. Since Aug 24 the 1st-shift job is opened after the shift ends, so it owns the following days.
  2. A job seen only in the posted list spreads its output evenly across the shift-days it owns. Weekly totals stay exact; a single day is a window average.
  3. A job seen in open-jobs exports is a series of snapshots. The pounds between two consecutive exports go to the shifts that ran in between (1st 7–3, 2nd 3–11, 3rd 11–7), split by hours when a shift straddles an export. Daily exports make every day exact; the shift under way at the last export shows as in progress.
  4. Checked against the last three weeks of hand-built workbooks: of shift-days match to the pound. The only miss is one job that was never posted.

Read with these in mind

  • Posted jobs plus whatever the last open-jobs export showed — output since that export is invisible until the next one.
  • Two other sites in the export (Ryder Midwest, PRN Southeast) are excluded.
  • Days after the last open-jobs export are awaiting; the shift in progress at that export is partial.
  • Guillotine output weights are included, so totals match the dashboard's "with Guillotine support" view, not its standard view.
  • No product breakdown — the export carries none. Mon–Fri only; a Saturday shift is inside the Friday-opened job, so Fridays can spike. Plant closure days (Good Friday, Memorial Day, Jul 3, Labor Day) are treated like weekends.
